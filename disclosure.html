Reader Disclosure

Golden Retriever Zone is reader-funded through affiliate programs. Some product links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission when you purchase through them. This page is our commitment to being transparent about that arrangement.

Affiliate Program Participation

Golden Retriever Zone participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and similar affiliate advertising programs. When you click a product link in one of our guides and complete a qualifying purchase, we receive a referral commission from Amazon. This commission comes from Amazon's own revenue — it is not added to your purchase price. You pay what Amazon charges, nothing more.

What We Recommend and Why

The Golden Zone Guide Editors recommend products in our guides because those products are relevant and useful for golden retriever owners — not because of the commission structure. We recommend products without affiliate links when those products are the best option. We exclude products that have affiliate links available when they are not genuinely useful for the breed.

No manufacturer, retailer, or brand pays to appear in our guides. Affiliate income is earned from Amazon based on completed purchases — not on any relationship with specific brands.

FTC Compliance

This disclosure is provided in accordance with Federal Trade Commission guidelines (16 CFR Part 255) regarding endorsements and material connections. As an Amazon Associate, Golden Retriever Zone earns from qualifying purchases.

Sponsored Content Policy

Golden Retriever Zone does not publish sponsored articles, paid product features, or advertorial content. All editorial decisions are made by the Golden Zone Guide Editors on the basis of what is most useful and accurate for golden retriever families. That position is not negotiable.
