Puppy and adolescent (0–2 years)

During this window, the dominant concern is developmental orthopedic disease. Golden Retriever health statistics by age 2026 data indicate that 18% of puppies show radiographic signs of hip dysplasia by 12 months. Adult (3–7 years)

This is the “golden middle”—but not free of hazards. Obesity is the hidden epidemic: nearly 40% of adult Goldens are overweight. Golden Retriever health statistics by age date from the UK Kennel Club shows that dogs in this age range have a 22% incidence of hypothyroidism. Senior and geriatric (8+ years)

After age 8, the epidemiological picture shifts dramatically. Golden Retriever health statistics by age history reveals that the breed’s cancer rate is among the highest in purebreds.
