🎯 How to socialize a golden retriever puppy activities

Socialisation isn’t just meeting dogs — it’s sounds, surfaces, people, and handling. Here are proven how to socialize a golden retriever puppy activities from our tribe:

  • Puppy playdates with one calm, vaccinated adult dog.
  • Car rides (start with 2 minutes, then 10).
  • Handling games: touch paws, ears, and mouth gently while rewarding.
  • Meet friendly neighbors (with treats).
  • Walk on different surfaces: grass, gravel, wood, tile.
